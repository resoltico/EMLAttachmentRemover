**Author of the drawings:** Claude (Anthropic AI model), working to the art direction and selections of Ervins Strauhmanis
**Date:** 5 October 2026
**Scope:**
- the status artwork in `macos-ui/Artwork.swift` (envelope and badge set)

## Statement

I drew the interface artwork in this package independently in this session, from explicit coordinates I wrote for this project. Every shape is listed numerically in `ARTWORK.md` → *Geometry*.

As its source, the artwork was **not** traced from, adapted from, reproduced from memory of, or derived from any of the following:
- Apple SF Symbols or any other Apple artwork
- Microsoft Fluent / Segoe icons
- Google Material Symbols
- Feather, Lucide, Font Awesome or Heroicons
- any other icon set, stock image, logo or third-party artwork

No files were downloaded or copied to create it.

This differs from an earlier exploration in the same session. The first drafts of the *application icon* reused the Feather Icons paperclip outline. That was disclosed when it was found, and the icon was replaced with independently drawn geometry. None of that superseded material is used here.

## Limits of this statement

- **Generic forms:** the envelope, check mark, exclamation mark, stop square and progress ring are conventional, generic forms. Mine were drawn independently, but they will inevitably resemble many existing icons, and no one can hold exclusive rights in those basic forms. What is specific to this work is the composition: the fixed envelope, the constant badge cut-out, the badge geometry and the snip mark.
- **Not legal certification:** this records how the work was made. It is not a legal certification of copyright ownership, non-infringement or trademark clearance, and it is not legal advice.
- **AI authorship:** copyright protection for AI-generated material is limited or uncertain in many jurisdictions, including the EU and the US. The project owner's documented creative direction and selection strengthens a claim, but does not guarantee one. For exclusive rights in a brand mark, registering a trademark is the dependable route.
